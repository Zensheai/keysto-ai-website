Why I Built Keys to AI

I started Keys to AI because I wanted to build something of my own—a media company that could give me freedom and flexibility. But as I dove in, I quickly realized I was drowning in tasks that had nothing to do with the creative work I actually wanted to do.

I knew AI could help, but everything I found online was either too technical, too expensive, or just another product pitch. So I started documenting my own journey—testing tools, building workflows, and figuring out what actually works on a solopreneur budget. Keys to AI is the resource I wish I had when I started.

My mission? Get you from overwhelmed to in control—at work and in life. You deserve to work ON your business, not just IN it. Let's make that happen—together.
